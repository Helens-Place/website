## What not to do

Do not give a long list of unrelated words on Monday and test them on Friday.
For a learner with literacy difficulties that is a weekly reminder of failure
that teaches almost nothing, because nothing connects the words to each other.

And do not correct every spelling in a piece of writing. If a child has written
something interesting, respond to what they wrote. Pick two or three spellings
worth working on, and leave the rest.

## For parents

Spelling is the area where well-meaning help most often turns into conflict.
If homework is going badly, it is entirely reasonable to stop, write a note to
the teacher, and protect the evening.

Persistent spelling difficulty alongside reading difficulty, particularly where
a child is obviously bright and articulate, is one of the clearer reasons to get
a proper assessment rather than keep guessing.
