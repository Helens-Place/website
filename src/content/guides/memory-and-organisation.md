## Why this matters more than it looks

Working memory is the desk space your brain works on. If the desk is small,
everything takes longer and things get knocked off the edge, regardless of how
clever the person is.

That is why forgetting is so often read as not caring. Someone who has forgotten
their kit for the fourth time this term looks careless. Usually they are
managing a system that is quietly overloaded, and often they are already
embarrassed about it.

## For teachers

Give one instruction at a time, or write them where they can be seen. Do not
make a child hold a question in mind while they queue for help. Allow processing
time after asking something, and resist filling the silence.

None of this slows a class down noticeably, and it removes a lot of avoidable
failure.

## For parents

Build the systems together rather than imposing them, and expect to adjust them.
A system that a child has helped design is one they might actually use.

And be careful about the language. Forgetting is not a character flaw. The
difference between "you never remember anything" and "let us find a way to make
this easier to remember" is, over years, enormous.
