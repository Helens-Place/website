This privacy policy explains what information I collect, how I protect any
information you submit to me, and how I use it.

I may amend this policy from time to time by updating this page. I suggest you
check this page occasionally to make sure you are aware of, and happy with, any
amendments.

## What information do I collect?

Personal information may be collected from you in various ways, for example:

1. Information about your computer and about your visits to and use of this
   website. This includes information about your browser, your IP address, your
   general location as determined from your IP address and provided by your
   browser, the site you came from, and the links followed when leaving the
   site. This information is also collected through cookies.
2. When you register and voluntarily provide information in response to
   questionnaires or download forms, or register for newsletters. This includes
   your name, email address, and any other details asked for and which you
   provide.
3. When you conduct activities on the site. For example, when you purchase a
   product or service I record what you purchased. I may collect your name and
   contact details, details of your subscription to my services, and other
   information gathered by Facebook and transferred to me, including your
   address.

## How is the information used?

I use this information to understand your needs and provide you with a better
service, and in particular for the following reasons:

- To supply to you services purchased via the website, and to process your
  payments.
- For statistical purposes and analysis for management purposes, in order to
  administer the website or improve my products and services.
- Internal record keeping and administrative purposes, and to inform you about
  events, services or products, or other related information that I think would
  be of interest to you, as explained above.
- To communicate marketing messages, newsletters and details of my business by
  post or email or similar technology. You can tell me at any time if you no
  longer require marketing communications.
- From time to time, I may also use your information to contact you for market
  research purposes, or to customise the website according to your interests.

Your information will not be disclosed to any third party unless you have given
your consent to such disclosure. You may at any time ask me to stop sending you
marketing messages by sending an email with the word UNSUBSCRIBE in the subject
box, or by telephoning me. If I contact you by email, each time you receive a
message you will also have the option to decline to receive further information
of that type.

If you would like me to destroy information I hold about you, please let me
know. Please note that if you use any of my services which require you to
provide personal information, deleting my records may mean that you need to
resubmit it to continue using such services.

I may disclose aggregate statistics about site visitors, clients and sales in
order to describe my services to prospective partners and other reputable third
parties, and for other lawful purposes. These statistics will include no
personally identifying information.

## Cookies

Cookies are text files stored on your computer, and accessible only to the
websites which create them.

This website may from time to time use cookies and log files for statistical
analysis, to understand user behaviour, to administer the site, to tailor the
information presented to a user based on their preferences, and to improve user
experience. Any information gathered by the use of cookies is compiled on an
aggregate, anonymous basis.

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies. You may delete or disable
cookies by following the instructions at
[allaboutcookies.org](http://www.allaboutcookies.org/manage-cookies/). Please
note that you may not be able to take full advantage of a website if you disable
cookies.

Further information about cookies can be found on the Interactive Advertising
Bureau's website, [www.allaboutcookies.org](http://www.allaboutcookies.org/).

## Social media

If you share content through social media, for example by liking Helen's Place
on Facebook or posting about it, those social networks will record that you have
done so and may set a cookie for this purpose.

In some cases, where a page on this website includes content from a social
network, those services may set a cookie even where you do not click a button.
As is the case for all cookies, I cannot access those set by social networks,
just as those social networks cannot access cookies I set myself.

## Log files

The systems behind this site automatically gather some anonymous information
about visitors, including IP addresses, browser type, language, and the times
and dates of webpage visits. The data collected does not include personally
identifiable information and is used, as described above, for statistical
analysis, to understand user behaviour, and to administer the site.

## Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people find useful. I chose it
deliberately, because it is the least intrusive option I could find.

It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not use
fingerprinting. It cannot follow you to other websites, and it does not build a
profile of you or recognise you if you come back. What I see are aggregate
counts: which pages were viewed, which site or search sent someone here, and a
broad breakdown by country, browser and device type. I cannot identify an
individual visitor from it.

Your IP address is used momentarily to work out roughly where a visit came from
and to filter out automated traffic. It is not stored against your visit and it
is not available to me.

Because this sets no cookies and does no tracking across sites, it does not need
your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. That
is why this site does not put a cookie banner in front of you. Cloudflare, Inc.
is a United States company and processes this data across its global network.
You can read exactly what it collects in
[Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).

## Security

The internet is not a secure medium. However, I take reasonable technical and
organisational precautions to prevent the loss, misuse or alteration of your
personal information.

I have put in place various security procedures as set out in this policy. For
example, security and privacy policies are periodically reviewed and enhanced as
necessary, and only authorised people have access to user information. While I
cannot ensure or guarantee that loss, misuse or alteration of data will not
occur, I use my best efforts to prevent this.

use of your personal information, I will use my best endeavours to contact you
by email to seek your consent to that use. Continued use of the service will
signify that you agree to any such changes.

described here. Owing to the global nature of the internet infrastructure, the

UK and the European Economic Area that do not have similar protections in place
regarding your data and its use. I have taken the steps outlined above to try to
improve the security of your information. By submitting your information you
consent to these transfers.

## Data protection statement, video submissions

I am collecting video submissions for use in the launch of Literacy Learning
Journeys: An Educator's Guide to Dyslexia, Ages 0-18. By submitting a video, you
agree to the following:

- **Purpose.** Your video will be used solely for the promotion of Literacy
  Learning Journeys: An Educator's Guide to Dyslexia, Ages 0-18, and may be
  combined with other submissions to create a longer piece responding to the
  prompt questions.
- **Use and editing.** Individual videos will not be altered, so no content will
  be added or removed, though they may be compiled into a larger video alongside
  other submissions.
- **Privacy.** You are not required to provide your full name in the video.
- **Data security and retention.** Videos will be stored securely and retained
  only for as long as necessary, in line with the Data Protection Act 2018 and
  UK data protection regulations.
- **Sharing and distribution.** Videos may be shared on social media platforms
  but will not be sold or passed on to third parties.
- **Editorial discretion.** I reserve the right not to use any submitted videos.
- **Legal compliance and consent.** By submitting a video, you confirm that you
  have the right to share its content and consent to its use as outlined above.

If you have any questions or wish to withdraw your consent, please contact me at
[helen@helensplace.co.uk](mailto:helen@helensplace.co.uk). Withdrawal will not
affect any actions taken before the written withdrawal is received.
