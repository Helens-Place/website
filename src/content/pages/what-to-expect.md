## How long is the report valid for?

Under the current regulations, the report is valid for life, because I hold an
Assessment Practising Certificate.

That means if your child later wants to apply for support, whether that is
Disabled Students' Allowance at university, Access to Work, or support in
college, they can use the report as part of the evidence base for the
application. They should not need to have it done again.

One honest caveat. Some universities and professional bodies are starting to
challenge that a little, and it does vary by institution. If you have questions
about how that applies to your situation, I am happy to talk it through.

## Why I do not separate dyslexia and dyscalculia assessments

I do not split dyscalculia and dyslexia assessments, because the two often
co-occur. Looking at literacy and numeracy together gives a truer picture of how
a person actually learns, rather than answering one question and leaving another
open.

This is also why the length of the assessment varies. If we are looking at
numeracy as well as literacy, it takes longer.

## What the assessment is not

It is not a test anyone passes or fails, and it is not something a child needs
to revise for. It is a structured way of finding out how someone learns, where
the sticking points are, and what will actually help.

Most children find the day far friendlier than they expected.
