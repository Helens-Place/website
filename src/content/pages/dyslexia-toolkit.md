## What you get

Clear, actionable strategies and a toolkit of evidence-informed technology to
support dyslexic learners, so they can access the curriculum, build confidence
and do well in the classroom.

It is written for the person who has to make it work on a Tuesday morning:
teachers, teaching assistants and SENCos, rather than procurement teams.

## Before you click

The guide is hosted by the publisher rather than on this site, and you download
it by giving them your email address. It is free, and you are giving your
