    question: What is a dyslexia assessment and what does it involve?
    answer: >-
      A full diagnostic assessment typically lasts two to three hours. I use
      standardised, nationally recognised tests to assess reading, writing,
      spelling, phonological processing, working memory and processing speed.
      You receive a comprehensive written report, clearly written and suitable
      for schools, universities and employers. Full assessments start from £450
      including VAT.

  - category: Assessments
    question: What is an exam access arrangement assessment?
    answer: >-
      An exam access arrangement assessment determines whether a student
      qualifies for accommodations in formal exams, such as extra time, a
      reader, a scribe, or use of a laptop. Schools need a qualified assessor to
      carry this out. I hold the Assessment Practising Certificate
      (23/APC05078) required for this work. These assessments start from £180
      including VAT.
  - category: Assessments
    question: What is the difference between a full assessment and an exam access arrangement?
    answer: >-
      A full diagnostic assessment gives a comprehensive picture of a learner's
      cognitive profile, identifies specific learning difficulties, and produces
      a detailed report. It suits diagnosis, EHCP applications, university
      applications and employer disclosure. An exam access arrangement
      assessment focuses specifically on whether a student meets the criteria
      for exam accommodations. It is narrower in scope and is used by schools
      for GCSE and A Level purposes. I offer both.
  - category: Assessments

    question: My child's school says it can arrange an assessment. Do I need to go privately?

      for you, I will say so.
