## Assessments

- A full diagnostic assessment is £450, and £150 secures the booking. The
  balance is payable on or before the day of the assessment.
- If you need to move the appointment, please give at least **7 days' notice**.
  The booking fee will be carried over to the new date.
- Where an assessment is cancelled or moved with **less than 7 days' notice**,
  the £150 booking fee is retained, because the day has been held and cannot
  usually be filled at short notice.
- **If your child is unwell on the day, tell me and we will simply rearrange.**
  I will not charge you for that. Assessing a child who is poorly produces an
  unreliable result and an unhappy child, which serves nobody.
- If I have to cancel or postpone, you will be offered the first available
  alternative date, or a full refund of everything paid, including the booking
  fee.
- Reports are issued within two weeks of the assessment.
- Exam access arrangement assessments and other assessments follow the same
  notice periods, with the booking fee set in proportion to the fee quoted.

## Tuition

- All lessons must be paid for in advance or at the time of the lesson.
- You must give at least **24 hours' notice** of a lesson cancellation,
  otherwise you will be charged the cost of the lesson. Lessons cancelled with
  less than 24 hours' notice are billed at no more than 1 hour of instruction.
- Where the tutor is absent, the client will not be billed, and any monies
  already paid will be credited to their next invoice.
- Scheduled tutorials will always take place on the day, time and venue
  specified, unless a tutorial is rearranged for a time that is mutually
  convenient to both tutor and student. Lessons continue until a mutually agreed
  termination date.
- Although I work within the school curriculum, I do not do homework or
  assignment questions that are meant to be the student's own work.

## Training and INSET

- Should you wish to cancel INSET, you must give at least **2 weeks' notice**,
  or you will be charged for the session. Further details of cancellation
  charges are set out on your invoice and quotation.
- Where a session is moved rather than cancelled, and a mutually convenient
  alternative date can be agreed within three months, no cancellation charge
  applies.
- Travel and accommodation costs already incurred and not recoverable remain
  payable, whatever the notice given.
- If I have to cancel, you will not be charged, and I will help you find an
  alternative date or, if you would prefer, another suitable presenter.

## Research and consultancy

- Quotations for research services are valid for **3 months**. After that
  period, a quotation will be redone and may be subject to a price increase.
- Unless otherwise stated, quotations represent the maximum price payable. Where
  projects are charged at an hourly or daily rate rather than a fixed price, the
  rate is clearly stated on the estimate.
- Where a project is cancelled after it has been accepted, all work completed up
  to the date of cancellation is chargeable, together with any non-recoverable
  costs already committed.
- In addition, where a project is cancelled with **less than 4 weeks' notice**
  of the agreed start date, **50%** of the remaining fee is payable, because the
  time has been reserved and cannot usually be reallocated at short notice.
- A project may be paused for up to **3 months** without charge, by agreement.
  Beyond that it is treated as a cancellation.
- Expert witness instructions are subject to separate terms, agreed in writing
  at the point of instruction.

## Speaking and events

- Fees, travel and any accommodation are agreed in writing before the booking is
  confirmed.
- If you cancel **more than 8 weeks** before the event, no fee is payable.
- If you cancel **between 4 and 8 weeks** before the event, **50%** of the
  agreed fee is payable.
- If you cancel **less than 4 weeks** before the event, the **full fee** is
  payable, because that date is unlikely to be filled.
- Travel and accommodation costs already incurred and not recoverable remain
  payable in all cases.
- If I have to cancel, no fee is payable, and I will do what I can to help you
  find a suitable replacement speaker.

## If circumstances make delivery impossible

Occasionally something happens that neither of us can control: serious illness,
bereavement, severe weather, transport failure, or a setting closing
unexpectedly. In those circumstances I will always try to rearrange rather than
charge, and I ask the same understanding in return. Nothing in these terms is
intended to hold you to a cancellation charge in genuinely exceptional
circumstances.
