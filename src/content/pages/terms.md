## Quotations and invoicing

- Quotations for research services are valid for a period of 3 months. After
  that period, a quotation will be redone and may be subject to a price
  increase.
- Unless otherwise stated, quotations for research services represent the
  maximum price payable. Where projects are charged at an hourly rate instead of
  a fixed price, the rate will be clearly stated on the estimate.
- All invoices must be paid within 14 days of the invoice date. Where they are
  not, they will be subject to a surcharge to cover any reasonable losses to
  Helen's Place.

## Payment

- All lessons must be paid for in advance or at the time of the lesson.
- I can accept cash, cheque, cards or BACS.

## Cancellation

- Should you wish to cancel INSET, you must give at least 2 weeks' notice, or
  you will be charged for the session. Further details of cancellation charges
  will be detailed on your invoice and quotation.
- You must give at least 24 hours' notice of lesson cancellation, otherwise you
  will be charged the cost of the lesson. Where the tutor is absent, the client
  will not be billed for the lesson, and any monies already paid will be
  credited to their next invoice.
- Lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice will be billed to the client
  at a rate of no more than 1 hour of instruction.

## Lessons

- Scheduled tutorials will always take place on the day, time and venue
  specified, unless a tutorial is rearranged for a time that is mutually
  convenient to both tutor and student. Lessons will continue until a mutually
  agreed termination date.
- Although I work within the school curriculum, I do not do homework or
  assignment questions that are meant to be the student's own work.
